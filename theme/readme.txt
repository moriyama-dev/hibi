Makes the Hibi Journal app the front page of the site.

== Description ==

With the Hibi Journal plugin, the journal app lives at /journal/ under any theme. This theme hands it the whole site instead: the front page opens straight into today's entry, and the app's screens move to the root (/calendar/, /d/2026-10-14/ ...). It also keeps the login on for a year and lands there after logging in.

Pages and posts that are not part of the app are shown in a plain reading layout. The theme requires the Hibi Journal plugin.
